Problem Discovery

The Problem

Why modern students struggle with placement readiness:

  • Ambiguous Job Roles: Postings are scattered, buzzword-heavy, and conflicting.
  • Zero Personalization: Students have no way to quantify what specific skills they lack.
  • Misguided Effort: Months wasted learning non-critical tools while missing prerequisites.
Problem Statement

Problem Statement

"There is no simple, unified tool that quantitatively tells a student exactly what skills they lack for a specific job and how to learn them — in one place."
Solution

Our Solution: SkillBridge

Bridging the gap with an end-to-end automated platform:

🎯 Target Role Selection

Pre-benchmarked against 2026 hiring standards across Data, Web, and AI.

⚡ Synonym Normalization

Standardizes aliases (e.g. postgres → SQL, js → JavaScript).

📊 Match Score Gauge

Dynamic visual breakdown of possessed vs. missing competencies.

🗺️ Prioritized Roadmap

Structured P1/P2/P3 learning steps linked to 100% free verified tutorials.

Objectives

Project Objectives

  • Accept target job roles and current technical skills from the user.
  • Perform intelligent synonym normalization and mathematical set-difference matching.
  • Deliver interactive Chart.js visualizations and readiness gauges.
  • Generate tailored, prioritized learning curricula with free learning resources.

Software Design

Resolves 40+ industry aliases to canonical skills.

Sorts missing competencies into P1, P2, and P3 milestones.
